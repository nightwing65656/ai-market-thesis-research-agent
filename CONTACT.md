# Request a Commercial License

This project is free for personal, educational and non-commercial use.
**Any business or production use requires a paid Commercial License** with
royalty terms -- see [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the full structure.

## How to reach out

1. **Preferred:** Open a "Commercial License Request" issue on this repo
   (Issues tab -> New Issue -> pick the Commercial License Request template).
2. **Direct contact:**
   - GitHub: [@nightwing65656](https://github.com/nightwing65656)
   - LinkedIn: [Dheeraj Krishna Kumar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dheeraj-krishna-kumar/)

## What happens next

- You'll get a response with an initial royalty/fee structure based on your
  use case (internal tool vs. client-facing product vs. resale carry
  different terms).
- Once terms are agreed, you'll receive a signed Commercial License
  Agreement -- only then are commercial rights granted (LICENSE Section 2).

## Before reaching out, have ready

- Your use case (internal tool, embedded in a product, resale, etc.)
- Expected scale (team size, user count or revenue attributable to this use)
- Your timeline
